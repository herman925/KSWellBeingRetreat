How to Use This Page

  1. View Resource Category: Click category header (with the down arrow) to expand and see all items in that category. The categories are expanded by default.
  2. Inventory vs. Boxes:
    • The left side (Inventory) shows the total quantities that SHOULD BE prepared.
    • The right side (Boxes) shows what has ALREADY been packed into specific boxes.
  3. Tracking Preparation Progress:
    • When you add items to boxes (right side), the remaining quantity on the left side will automatically decrease.
    • Example: If you need 10 name tags (left side), and you pack 6 into a box (right side), the left side will show 4 remaining to be prepared.
  4. Update Quantities: Click on any number to edit the quantity. Press Enter or click elsewhere when done.
  5. Export/Import Data: Click the corresponding button to download or upload inventory data as a CSV file.

Important: Changes to quantities are NOT SAVED automatically. Make sure to export your data if you want to save all changes permanently.
